## NTC10D-11


- NTC10D-11

- Type: Negative Temperature Coefficient (NTC) Thermistor
- Resistance: 10 kΩ at 25°C
- B Value: ~3435 K (typical)
- Max Voltage: 250V AC
- Max Current: 2.5A
- Operating Temperature: −40°C to +125°C
- Tolerance: ±5% (at 25°C)
- Application: Inrush current limiter, temperature sensing



## MF72-2.5D11


The `MF72-2.5D11` (also written as MF72 2.5D11 or MF72-002.5D11) is a Power NTC (Negative Temperature Coefficient) Thermistor used primarily as an Inrush Current Limiter (ICL) in electronic power circuits.
