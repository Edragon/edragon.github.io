

# kangxi-dat


KCT8539SD-1 is a highly integrated RF Front-End Integrated Circuit incorporates key RF functionality needed for IEEE 802.11a/n/ac/ax WLAN systems operating in the 5.15-5.85GHz range. 

KCT8539SD-1 integrates a highefficiency high-linearity power amplifier (PA), a low noise amplifier (LNA) with bypass, the associated matching network, LO rejection and harmonic filters all in one device.
