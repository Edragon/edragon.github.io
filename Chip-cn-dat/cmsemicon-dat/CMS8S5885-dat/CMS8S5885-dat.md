

# CMS8S5885-dat

- [[CMS8S6990-dat]] - [[CMS8S5885-dat]] - [[Cmsemicon-dat]]


The CMS8S5885 is an 8-bit 8051-core flash microcontroller manufactured by Cmsemicon (CMSEMICON), designed for motor control, appliances, and general embedded applications.

Key Specifications

- • Core & Speed: 8-bit 8051 core running up to 24 MHz
- • Memory: 16 KB Flash ROM, 1 KB Data Flash, 256 B RAM + 1 KB XRAM
- • Operating Voltage: 2.1 V – 5.5 V
- • Operating Temperature: -40°C to 105°C
- • Packages: TSSOP20, QFN20
- • Analog & Timers: Up to 18 channels of 12-bit ADC, 6 channels of enhanced PWM (suitable for bridge control), 5×16-bit timers, and a buzzer driver
- • Interfaces & I/O: Up to 18 GPIOs, 2× UART, 1× SPI, 1× I2C
