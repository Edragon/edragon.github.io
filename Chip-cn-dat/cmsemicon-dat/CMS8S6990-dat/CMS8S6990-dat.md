The CMS8S6990 is an enhanced 1T 8051-core 8-bit microcontroller manufactured by Cmsemicon.

Key Specifications & Features

• Core & Speed: Enhanced 1T 8051 core running at up to 24MHz (48MHz peripheral operation).
• Memory: 16KB Flash, 1KB Data Flash, 256B + 1KB RAM, plus a 96-bit unique ID (UID).
• Operating Voltage & Temp: 2.1V to 5.5V operating voltage range; industrial temperature rating of -40°C to +105°C (or up to 85°C/125°C junction limits depending on package specs).
• Peripherals:
	• Up to 22 GPIOs with digital function remapping
	• Up to 22-channel 12-bit ADC
	• 2 analog comparators, 2 operational amplifiers (OPAs), and 1 programmable gain amplifier (PGA)
	• 6-channel enhanced PWM with dead-zone control
	• 5 16-bit timers
	• Communication interfaces: 2 UARTs, 1 SPI, and 1 I2C
	• Built-in LVR (Low-Voltage Reset), LVD (Low-Voltage Detector), WDT, LSE/WUT, and beeper.
• Packages: Available in TSSOP20, QFN20, SSOP24, and QFN24 variants (including related family models like CMS8S6980 and CMS8S6990N).
• Development Tools: Supported by Keil C51 IDE, CMS Writer, and CMS-ICE8 OB/PRO.
