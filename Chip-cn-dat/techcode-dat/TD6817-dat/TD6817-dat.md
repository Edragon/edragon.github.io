



# TD6817-dat

- [[techcode-dat]] - [[TD6817-dat]] - [[dcdc-down-dat]] == 5.5V 2A 


The TD6817 (manufactured by Techcode) is a 1.5 MHz, 2A synchronous step-down (buck) regulator IC in a compact SOT-23-5 / TSOT23-5 package.

Key Specifications

• Input Voltage Range: 2.5V to 5.5V (suited for single-cell Li-Ion or 5V rails)
• Max Output Current: Up to 2A
• Switching Frequency: 1.5 MHz constant frequency (current-mode architecture)
• Output Options: Available as an adjustable version (0.6V feedback reference) or fixed output voltage versions
• Low Dropout / Efficiency: Synchronous rectification (no external Schottky diode needed) with 100% duty cycle capability and burst mode at light loads
• Protections: Overtemperature and overcurrent protection
• Package: SOT-23-5 / TSOT23-5
