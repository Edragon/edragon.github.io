


# 203B-dat

- [[203B-dat]] - [[xinyongcheng-dat]] - [[sensor-PIR-dat]] - [[Wireless-Video-Doorbell-dat]]



The 203B-S0 (also listed as XYC-PIR203B-S0 or 203B by manufacturer Xinyongcheng) is a dual-element pyroelectric infrared (PIR) motion sensor element in a 3-pin TO-5 metal package. It outputs a small analog signal and requires an external signal-conditioning circuit (such as an amplifier and comparator) plus a Fresnel lens to detect motion.

Key Specifications

- • Package: 3-pin TO-5 metal can
- • Window size: 4 × 3 mm (with dual 2 × 1 mm sensitive elements)
- • Spectral response: 5.5 – 14 μm
- • Supply voltage: 2 – 15 V
- • Sensitivity: ≥ 3000 V/W
- • Signal output (Vp-p): ≥ 3500 mV (under test conditions: 420K blackbody, ~1 Hz modulation, 72.5 dB gain)
- • Noise (Vp-p): < 80 mV
- • Source (offset) voltage: 0.4 – 1.0 V
- • Operating temperature: −30°C to +70°C
