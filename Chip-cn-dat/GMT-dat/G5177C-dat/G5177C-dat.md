



# G5177C-dat

- [[DCDC-boost-dat]] - [[GMT-dat]] - [[G5177C-dat]] - [[ZXW602-dat]] - [[power-bank-dat]]

`G5177C` is the top-side SMD marking for the `G5177CF11U`, a synchronous step-up (boost) DC-DC converter IC made by Global Mixed-mode Technology (GMT). The S40B code printed alongside it is a manufacturing lot, date, or batch tracking code rather than a separate part number.

Key Specifications

• Function. Synchronous rectifier step-up (boost) converter designed to step up a single-cell Li-ion/polymer battery (around 3.0V–4.2V) to a higher regulated voltage like `5V`.
• Output Current. Delivers up to 3A guaranteed output current at 5V from a 3.3V input.
• Switching Frequency. Operates at a fixed 500 kHz PWM frequency under normal loads and automatically skips pulses at light loads to save power.
• Efficiency & Power. Achieves up to 90% efficiency (at 2A load) with a low 110 µA typical quiescent current and under 1 µA shutdown current.
• Package. Housed in an SOP-8 (FD) surface-mount package with an exposed thermal pad underneath.
