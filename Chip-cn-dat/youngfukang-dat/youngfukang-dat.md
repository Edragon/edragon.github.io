The CS83702E (also referenced as CS83702) is an 18W mono Class D audio power amplifier integrated circuit (IC).

Key Features and Specifications

• Type: Mono Class D / Class R audio power amplifier with a built-in boost module.
• Power Supply: Designed for single-cell lithium battery-powered applications (such as a 3.7V supply).
• Output Power: Delivers up to 18W of constant power into low-impedance speakers (down to 2Ω) when boosted.
• Modes & Functions: Features AB/D switching mode to minimize FM interference in audio subsystems, a mute function, and three anti-breaking sound modes.
• Package Type: Typically available in an EQA16 or SOP-16 surface-mount package.
