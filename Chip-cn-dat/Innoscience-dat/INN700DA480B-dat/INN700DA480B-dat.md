


# INN700DA480B-dat

- [[Innoscience-dat]] - [[INN700DA480B-dat]] == 700V 480 mΩ - [[transistor-dat]] - [[transistor-power-dat]] 


The INN700DA480B is a 700V GaN-on-Silicon enhancement-mode power transistor manufactured by Innoscience.

Key specifications

- • Component type. Normally-off enhancement-mode Gallium Nitride (GaN) HEMT power switch.
- • Voltage rating. 700 V maximum drain-source voltage (\(V_{DS}\)).
- • On-resistance. Approximately 480 mΩ typical \(R_{DS(on)}\) (indicated by the "480" in the part numbering scheme, alongside sibling parts like the 190 mΩ INN700DA190B and 240 mΩ INN700DA240B).
- • Package. DFN 5 mm × 6 mm surface-mount package (denoted by the "DA" package code).
- • Switching performance. Features zero reverse-recovery charge (\(Q_{rr}\)) and very low gate/output charge for high-frequency switching.
