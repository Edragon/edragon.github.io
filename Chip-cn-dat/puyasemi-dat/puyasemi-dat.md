The PUYA `P25Q40H` is an ultra-low-power, 4-Megabit (4M-bit / 512KB) serial NOR flash memory chip manufactured by Puya Semiconductor Co., Ltd.

Key Specifications & Features

• Capacity: 4M-bit (512 KB), part of the `P25Q40H`/20H/10H/05H family (scaling down to 2M, 1M, and 512K-bit)
• Operating Voltage: 2.30V – 3.60V
• Interface: SPI, Dual I/O, Quad I/O
• Clock Frequency: Up to 104 MHz / 108 MHz
• Power Consumption:
	• Deep power-down: ~0.6 µA
	• Standby: ~9 µA
• Performance:
	• Page Program: ~2 ms
	• Erase (page, sector, block, chip): ~8 ms
