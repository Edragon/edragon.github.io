



# TP2124-dat

- [[TP2124-dat]] - [[amplifier-dat]] - [[Wireless-Video-Doorbell-dat]] - [[xiaomi-dat]] - [[3peak-dat]] - [[amplifier-transimpedance-dat]] 


`B24T` is the SMD top-marking code for the `3PEAK` `TP2124` (specifically the TP2124-TR in a TSSOP14 package), a nanopower, rail-to-rail I/O quad operational amplifier.

Key specifications

- • Configuration. Quad-channel operational amplifier (4 op-amps in one chip).
- • Package. TSSOP14 (14-pin Thin Shrink Small Outline Package).
- • Supply voltage. 1.8 V to 6.0 V single supply (or ±0.9 V to ±3.0 V dual supply).
- • Quiescent current. 600 nA typical (950 nA max) per channel.
- • **Gain-bandwidth product (GBWP)**. 18 kHz.
- • **Slew rate**. 10 mV/μs.
- • Input offset voltage. 1.5 mV max (0.5 μV/°C drift).
- • Operating temperature. -40°C to 125°C.

Common applications

• Battery-powered and solar-powered portable devices.
• Wireless remote sensors and energy-scavenging sensor networks.
• Environmental, gas, and oxygen sensors.
• Low-power active filters and current sensing.
