


# TP102-dat

- [[3peak-dat]] - [[TP102-dat]] - [[Wireless-Video-Doorbell-dat]] - [[xiaomi-dat]] - [[amplifier-dat]]


The `3PEAK` `TP102` (often listed as TP10-2-SR or TP102-SR) is a dual-channel, rail-to-rail input/output operational amplifier designed for low-voltage and precision analog signal conditioning.

Key Specifications

• Channels: 2 (dual op-amp)
• Supply Voltage: 2.7 V to 5.5 V (single supply)
• Gain-Bandwidth Product (GBW): 6 MHz
• Slew Rate: 4.5 V/μs
• Quiescent Current: ~600 μA per channel
• Output Current: Up to 100 mA
• Input Offset Voltage (\(V_{OS}\)): ±3 mV max
• Input Offset Voltage Drift: ~1–2 μV/°C
• Input Bias / Offset Current: ~1 pA (at 25°C)
• Input Voltage Noise Density: 19 nV/√Hz at 1 kHz
• CMRR: Up to 100 dB
• Operating Temperature: -40°C to +125°C
• Packages: SOP-8 (TP10-2-SR / TP102-SR) and MSOP-8 (TP102-VR)

Common Applications & Alternatives

• Typical Uses: Motor control (including e-bikes), portable/consumer audio, capacitive load/LCD driving, and buffering CMOS DACs or ASICs.
• Listed Alternatives: TP1562AL1-SR, TP1562AL1-SO1R-S, TP2412-SR, TPA6582-SO1R, and TPA6582Q-SO1R-S.
