## HX4004 


HX-JE

The HX-JE (often marked as HX=JE) is a small 6-pin surface-mount (SMD) integrated circuit in a SOT23-6 package, commonly known as the HX4004 or HX4004A DC-DC boost converter.

Key Details

• Function: It is a step-up (boost) DC-DC regulator designed to take a lower input voltage (such as from a single-cell lithium battery around 2V–3.7V) and boost it to a steady 5V output.
• Common Use: It is widely used in portable electronics like Bluetooth speakers (e.g., Fiddler speakers) and battery-powered devices to power USB ports, display logic, or internal memory.
• Common Failure Mode: It is often the first component to burn out or short-circuit if a battery is connected with reversed polarity or if the device experiences a power surge, causing the device to fail to power on or drop USB voltage (e.g., dropping to around 2.8V instead of 5V).
• Equivalent Parts: It is functionally similar to or interchangeable with regulators like the HX4004A. 



`HX JE`

The `HX4004` (commonly marked as HX=JE or HX4004A-MFC) is a low-noise, constant-frequency switched-capacitor DC/DC boost converter. It is widely utilized in battery-powered devices to safely step up a low input voltage (2.8V to 5.0V) into a regulated, stable 5V output.



Applications
- White LED Backlighting
- Li-lon Battery Backup Supplies
- Local 3V to 5V Conversion
- Smart Card Readers
- PCMCIA Local 5V Supplies

The HX4004 is a low noise, constant frequency (400kHz) switched capacitor voltage doubler. It produces a regulated output voltage from a 2.8V to 5.0V input. Low external parts count (one flying capacitor and two small bypass capacitors at VIN and VOUT) make the HX4004 ideally suited for small, battery-powered applications.

The HX4004 have thermal shutdown capability and can survive a continuous short circuit from VOUT to GND. Built-in soft-start circuitry prevents excessive inrush current during start-up.

High switching frequency enables the use of small ceramic capacitors. A low current shutdown feature disconnects the load from VIN and reduces quiescent current to <1uA. The HX4004 is available in the SOT-23-6L power packages.
