


# huayi-dat

- [[mosfet-dat]] - [[huayi-dat]] - [[HYG015N10NS1TA-dat]] - [[TOLL-dat]] - [[ESC-dat]] - [[PCB-footprint-dat]]

The HYG015N10NS1TA is a 100V, 380A N-channel enhancement-mode power MOSFET in a TOLL-8L package manufactured by Huayi Microelectronics.

Key Specification
- • Drain-to-Source Voltage (\(V_{DSS}\)): 100V
- • Continuous Drain Current (\(I_{D}\)): 380A at \(T_C = 25^\circ\text{C}\) (pulsed up to 1200A)
- • On-State Resistance (\(R_{DS(on)}\)): 1.2 mΩ typical (1.5 mΩ max) at \(V_{GS} = 10\text{V}\), \(I_D = 100\text{A}\)
- • Gate-to-Source Voltage (\(V_{GS}\)): ±20V max; threshold voltage (\(V_{GS(th)}\)) 2–4V
- • Total Gate Charge (\(Q_{g}\)): 205 nC at \(V_{GS} = 10\text{V}\)
- • Power Dissipation (\(P_{D}\)): 428.5W at \(T_C = 25^\circ\text{C}\)
- • Operating Temperature: -55°C to +175°C
- • Package: TOLL-8L (RoHS compliant, halogen-free, 100% avalanche tested)

Typical Applications

- • Switching power supplies
- • Power management for inverter systems
- • Battery management systems (BMS)
