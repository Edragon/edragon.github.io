


# IP2133-dat

IP2133 - [[IP2133-dat]] - [[injoinic-dat]] - [[emarker-dat]] - [[SW3516-dat]]


The IP2133 is an electronic marker (E-Marker) integrated circuit from Injoinic used in USB Type-C cables to communicate cable capabilities—such as power ratings, data support, and vendor identity—to connected devices.
