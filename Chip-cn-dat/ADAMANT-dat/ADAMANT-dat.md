


# ADAMANT-dat

- [[AD30N54D3-dat]] - [[ADAMANT-dat]] - [[IP2726-dat]]


The `AD30N54D3` is a 30V N-channel MOSFET (SGT technology) made by ADAMANT (Astatine / 砹德曼) in a PPAK 3x3 (DFN3x3) surface-mount package.

Key Specifications

- • Type: N-channel MOSFET (SGT)
- • Drain-Source Voltage (\(V_{DS}\)): 30V
- • Continuous Drain Current (\(I_{D}\)): 54A
- • On-State Resistance (\(R_{DS(on)}\)): 5.7 mΩ (typical at higher gate drive; ~8.4 mΩ at lower gate drive)
- • Package: PPAK 3x3 / DFN3x3

Common Applications

• USB Power & Fast Chargers: Used as a VBUS switch or synchronous buck MOSFET in USB-C/USB-A chargers (such as those covered in ChargerLAB teardowns).
• Motor Drives: Used in three-phase bridge drives and ESCs, including vacuum cleaner motors and drone ESCs.
