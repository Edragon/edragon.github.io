## FU6861Q

`FU6861Q` (often labeled with variations or mistaken for an FH series) from Fortior Technology

1. Fortior Technology: FU6861Q (Motor Driver MCU)
   
If your part is a motor controller or driver chip, it is the FU6861Q manufactured by Fortior Technology.

Package: QFN-56 (Quad Flat No-leads).

Function: A highly integrated, dual-core 3-phase brushless DC (BLDC) / PMSM motor control MCU. It pairs an 8051 core with a dedicated hardware Motor Control Engine (ME).

Common Applications: High-speed electric tools, garden equipment, drones (ESCs), and vacuum cleaners.
