The XMC QH128AHIG (officially XM25QH128AHIG) is a 128 Mbit (16 MB) Serial Flash memory chip manufactured by Wuhan Xinxin Semiconductor Manufacturing Corp. (XMC).

Key Specifications

• Capacity: 128 Mbit / 16 Megabytes (65,536 programmable pages of 256 bytes)
• Operating Voltage: 2.7V to 3.6V (3V class)
• Interface: Standard SPI, Dual SPI, Quad SPI, and QPI
• Package Type: SOP-8 (208-mil wide body)
• Endurance: 100,000 program/erase cycles typical
• Data Retention: 20 years
• Common Uses: PC/laptop BIOS chips, network equipment, set-top boxes, and IoT devices.
