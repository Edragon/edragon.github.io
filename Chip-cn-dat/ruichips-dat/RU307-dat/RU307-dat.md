


# RU307-dat

- [[RU307-dat]] - [[ruichips-dat]] - [[mosfet-dat]]

The RU3070L (often referred to as RU307 / RU3070) is an N-channel power MOSFET manufactured by Ruichips Semiconductor Co., Ltd.

Key Specifications (RU3070L)

• Type: N-Channel Power MOSFET
• Drain-Source Voltage (\(V_{DSS}\)): 30 V
• Continuous Drain Current (\(I_{D}\)): 70 A (up to 100A depending on package/heatsink rating)
• Drain-Source On-Resistance (\(R_{DS(ON)}\)):
	• Typ. 3.3 mΩ @ \(V_{GS} = 10\text{V}\)
	• Typ. 5.0 mΩ @ \(V_{GS} = 4.5\text{V}\)
• Package Type: TO-252 (D-Pak) surface-mount package
• Common Applications: Load switches, DC-DC converters, power management, and battery protection circuits.
