4V-60V 1A Non-Synchronous Step-Down DCDC Converter
With High Efficiency Sleep Mode


The top marking 1600A on a 6-pin SOT-23 (TSOT-23-6 / SOT23-6L) package typically identifies a variant of the LMR16006 (such as LMR16006XDDCR / LMR16006XDDCT), a step-down (buck) DC-DC switching regulator.

Key Specifications (LMR16006X Series)

- • Function: Non-synchronous step-down (buck) converter IC
- • Input Voltage: 4 V to 60 V
- • Output / Switch Rating: Rated for up to 600 mA output current (some variant datasheets list up to 1 A switch/converter rating depending on exact revision)
- • Switching Frequency: Fixed 600 kHz
- • Package: 6-pin SOT-23 / TSOT-23-6
- • Pinout (SOT23-6): 1. BOOT, 2. GND, 3. FB, 4. EN, 5. VIN, 6. SW
