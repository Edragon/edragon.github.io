The ST20100L is a 100V, 20A Schottky barrier diode in a surface-mount TO-277 package, produced by various manufacturers (such as FUXINSEMI, CJ, MSKSEMI, and YINT).

Key Specifications

- • Reverse Voltage (\(V_{R}\) / \(V_{RRM}\)): 100V max
- • Average Rectified Forward Current (\(I_{O}\)): 20A
- • Peak Forward Surge Current (\(I_{FSM}\)): 280A (per CJ and YINT listings)
- • Forward Voltage Drop (\(V_{F}\)): ~0.65V–0.72V @ 20A (varies by manufacturer)
- • Package: TO-277 (SMD)
- • Operating Junction Temperature: -55°C to +150°C (per CJ listing)
