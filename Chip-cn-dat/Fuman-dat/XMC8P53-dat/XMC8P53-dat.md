The XMC8P53 (such as the XMC8P53S8) is an 8-bit One-Time Programmable (OTP) microcontroller manufactured by Shenzhen Fuman Elec.

Key Specifications

• Core: 8-bit
• CPU Speed: Up to 16 MHz
• Program Memory: 1 KB OTP
• RAM Size: 48 Bytes
• Operating Voltage: 2.0V to 5.5V
• I/O Pins: 6 general-purpose I/O pins
• Package: SOP-8
• Operating Temperature: -40°C to +85°C
• Built-in Features: Power-on reset (POR), undervoltage detection (BOR/LVD/LVR/LVI), watchdog reset, external pin reset (NRST), and general-purpose timers.

Common Applications

• Frequently used in compact consumer electronics, such as 5V USB ultrasonic mist maker and humidifier modules (where it drives high-frequency drive pulses to a MOSFET and piezo atomizer disc).
