The HC2015 in an SOP-8 package is a surface-mount DC-DC buck converter / power management IC commonly used in car chargers, USB charging modules, and adjustable/fast-charging power circuits. 

Note that "JW" often appears on listings alongside similar power-management ICs (such as JoulWatt/JW series buck converters or distributor part labels), while the HC2015 itself is widely listed under HCW / Hengcheng Micro.

Key Specifications & Characteristics

- • Package: SOP-8 (8-pin surface-mount SMD)
- • Function: DC-DC step-down (buck) conversion / charging power IC
- • Typical Output & Charging Use: Frequently paired with fast-charging / protocol controllers (such as QC3.0 or USB PD modules) and listed around 5V / up to ~3.4A–3.6A depending on the specific variant and circuit design.
- • Protections: Built-in over-voltage, over-current, and thermal shutdown protection.
- • Common Applications: Automotive car chargers, USB adapters, multi-port fast chargers, and compact DC-DC power modules.
