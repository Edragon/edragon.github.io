## relevant 

"8055 MSOP8" typically refers to integrated circuits (ICs) with the 8055 part number in an 8-pin Mini Small Outline Package (MSOP-8), such as the AW8055BMPR audio amplifier or the AD8055 family of high-speed operational amplifiers.

Common ICs matching 8055 MSOP8

• `AW8055BMPR` (Awinic). A Class-D audio power amplifier packaged in an MSOP-8-EP (MSOP-8 with an exposed thermal pad).

• `AD8055` / `AD8056` (Analog Devices). High-speed, 300 MHz voltage feedback operational amplifiers. While the single-channel AD8055 comes in SOT-23-5 and SOIC-8 packages, its dual-channel counterpart, the AD8056ARMZ, uses the MSOP-8 (RM-8) package.

• `SGM8052` / `SGM8055` (SG Micro). High-speed 250 MHz CMOS operational amplifiers. Note that in this series, the SGM8052 comes in MSOP-8, whereas the SGM8055 uses an MSOP-10 package.
