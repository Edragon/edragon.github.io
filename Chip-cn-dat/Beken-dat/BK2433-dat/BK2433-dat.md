The BK2433 most commonly refers to a 2.4 GHz wireless System-on-Chip (SoC) from Beken Corporation featuring an 8051-compatible microcontroller and USB 2.0 support. It can also appear as a model or part number on unrelated items, such as automotive brake kits or appliance control boards.

Beken BK2433 Wireless SoC

- • RF & Wireless: Operates in the worldwide 2.4 GHz ISM band using FSK/GFSK modulation with 250 kbps, 1 Mbps, or 2 Mbps data rates, programmable output power (-40 dBm to +5 dBm), and 6 data pipes for 1:6 star networks.
- • MCU & Memory: Integrates a Bk51 MCU (8051-compatible), 32 KB OTP program memory, protective mask ROM, 1 KB MTP customer data memory, 256 B IRAM, and 2 KB SRAM.
- • Interfaces & Power: Includes a 12 MHz USB 2.0 full-speed controller, three timers/counters, I2C, SPI, and UART, running on a 1.9 V to 3.6 V supply.
- • Packaging & Uses: Available in QFN packages (such as QFN32 / BK2433MB and 56-pin QFN / BK2433KB), commonly used in wireless peripherals like remotes and keyboards. You can review the BK2433 Datasheet for full pinouts and electrical specs.
