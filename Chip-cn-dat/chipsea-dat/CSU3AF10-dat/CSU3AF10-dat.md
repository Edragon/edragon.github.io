


# CSU3AF10-dat

- [[chipsea-dat]] - 3AF10-QN28 - [[CSU3AF10-dat]] - [[SC8933-dat]] - [[fast-charge-protocols-dat]] - [[charger-USB-dat]] - [[power-bank-dat]] 

3AF10-QN28 is the surface-mount marking for the Chipsea CSU3AF10 (in a QFN-28 package), a USB Type-C and fast-charging protocol controller IC. You can review its technical specifications, overview, and software resources on the official Chipsea CSU3AF10 page.

Key Specifications

• Manufacturer: Chipsea Technologies
• Package: QFN-28
• Core & Memory: 8-bit RISC microcontroller core (up to 24 MHz) with built-in Flash memory
• Operating Voltage: 2.2 V – 5.5 V
• Operating Temperature: -40 °C to 85 °C ambient (-40 °C to 105 °C junction)
• Low-Power Modes: ~3.5 mA normal operating current (at 12 MHz, 3.3 V) and < 10 µA sleep mode

Common Applications

• Power banks and portable energy storage
• USB-C fast chargers and car chargers
• USB hubs, docks, and monitors
