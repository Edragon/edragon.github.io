CSU32P10 is an 8-bit CMOS single-chip RISC MCU with a 12-bit ADC and built-in 2K x 14-bit OTP program memory


The Chipsea CSU32P10-MSOP10 (often searched as "32P10-MS") is an 8-bit CMOS RISC microcontroller (MCU) in a 10-pin MSOP package with a 12-bit ADC and one-time programmable (OTP) memory.

Key Specifications

- • CPU & Memory: 8-bit RISC core, 2K × 14-bit OTP program memory, 64-byte SRAM, 8-level stack, and 42 single-word instructions.
- • Clock: Internal 16 MHz oscillator (±1% accuracy at 5 V, 25 °C).
- • Analog & Peripherals: 12-bit ADC (5 external + 3 special channels) with selectable internal/external voltage references (1.4 V, 2.0 V, 3.0 V, 4.0 V, VDD) and digital comparator, plus PWM and buzzer outputs.
- • Power & Operating Range: 2.4 V to 5.5 V supply voltage, -40 °C to +85 °C operating temperature, ~0.8 mA in normal mode (16 MHz oscillator / 4 MHz CPU at 3 V), and <1 µA in hibernation mode.
- • Package: MSOP10 (part of the CSU32P10 family, which also includes SOP8, SOP14, and TSSOP14).
