


# CSU38M20-dat

- [[chipsea-dat]] - [[CSU38M20-dat-dat]] - [[MCU-dat]] - [[power-bank-dat]] - [[SW6106-dat]] 

The marking `CSU38M20` (or 38M20) on a Chipsea Technologies integrated circuit refers to the CSU38M20, an 8-bit RISC microcontroller (MCU). Because of limited space on small chip packages, Chipsea abbreviates the full part number on the IC's top marking (for example, 38M20-QN24 for the QFN24 package).
