


# AT32F421-dat


- [[AT32F421-dat]] - [[artery-dat]] 

The `AT32F421` is a value-line 32-bit microcontroller from Artery Technology built around an ARM Cortex-M4 core running at up to 120 MHz with DSP instructions. It is designed for industrial control, motor applications, and mixed-signal processing.

Key Specifications

- • Core & Clock: ARM Cortex-M4 up to 120 MHz, MPU, DSP instructions, and an internal 48 MHz factory-trimmed RC oscillator.
- • Memory: 16 to 64 KB Flash (plus 4 KB boot memory), 8 to 16 KB SRAM, and a password-protected Security Library (sLib) area.
- • Analog & Timers: 12-bit, 15-channel ADC (up to 2 MSPS), two high-speed rail-to-rail comparators, one 16-bit advanced timer, five 16-bit general-purpose timers, and a 5-channel DMA controller.
- • Connectivity & I/O: Up to 2x USART, 2x SPI (multiplexed I²S), and 2x I²C, with mostly 5 V-tolerant I/Os.
- • Power & Packages: 2.4 V–3.6 V (or 2.6 V–3.6 V per product page) operating range, -40°C to +105°C temperature rating, and packages including LQFP48, LQFP32, QFN32, QFN28, and mini TSSOP20.
