The FMD FT61EC (from Fremont Micro Devices) is an 8-bit A/D-type microcontroller series designed for applications requiring analog-to-digital conversion and sensor signal processing.

Key Specifications

- • Architecture: 8-bit RISC core with 37 instructions and selectable 2T/4T instruction cycles (minimum cycle time of 125 ns at VDD > 2.7V).
- • Operating Voltage: 2.0V to 5.5V, with a low-power SLEEP mode and under-voltage detection.
- • Memory: 2K × 14-bit program memory, 128 × 8-bit data RAM, 256 × 8-bit data EEPROM, and an 8-layer hardware stack.
- • Clocks: Built-in 16 MHz high-speed RC oscillator (HIRC), 32 kHz low-speed RC oscillator (LIRC), external clock/crystal support, and crystal clock miss detection.
- • Peripherals & I/O: Multi-channel ADC, up to 14 bi-directional I/Os (with configurable pull-up/pull-down and PA0–PA7 port change interrupts), 8-bit Timer0, 16-bit Timer1 (capture/compare), and 8-bit Timer2 (complementary PWM with deadband).
- • Packages & Programming: Available in packages such as SOP8, SOP14, SOP16, SOP18, and DIP8 (depending on specific sub-part like FT61EC21B or FT61EC23), with support for in-system programming (ISP) and in-circuit debugging.
