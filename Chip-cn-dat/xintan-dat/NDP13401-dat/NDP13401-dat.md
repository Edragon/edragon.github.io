



# NDP13401-dat

- [[xintan-dat]] - [[NDP13401-dat]] - [[dcdc-down-dat]] == 4A 30V - [[NDP-semi-dat]]

The NDP13401KC is a 4.0A, 30V high-efficiency synchronous step-down (buck) DC/DC converter integrated circuit manufactured by NDP (Jiangsu Xintan Microelectronics). Designed for power management applications, it integrates both high-side and low-side power MOSFETs into a compact SOP-8 package.
