## info 

- [[Ingenic-dat]] - [[T31-dat]] 


The Ingenic T31 is a low-power System-on-Chip (SoC) built on a 22nm process, widely used in smart IP cameras, webcams, and embedded AIoT devices.

Key Specifications

- • CPU: 1.0GHz–1.4GHz XBurst1 (MIPS-based) core
- • AI Accelerator: 0.5 TOPS@int8 vector deep learning engine
- • ISP & Video: Tiziano 3.0 ISP supporting H.265/H.264 encoding up to 5MP@25fps, with fast boot (~200ms Linux startup / video output)
- • Power: ~450mW at 1080p@25fps
- • Display & Camera Input: Smart LCD controller (up to 800x600@60Hz), MIPI CSI (2-lane) and DVP camera interfaces
- • Audio: Integrated audio codec (up to 96kHz), I2S, echo cancellation, and support for a 4-channel digital mic array
- • Connectivity & Peripherals: USB-OTG, GMAC, SDIO, UART, I2C, SPI, PWM, ADC, GPIO, WDT
- • Package: QFN / BGA

Common Uses & Community

- • Commercial Cameras: Used in budget and smart home cameras (such as the Wyze Cam V3) for low-light imaging and onboard or cloud AI detection. Users on r/wyzecam note a consensus that the T31 provides capable onboard AI and low-light ISP features, though some capabilities are gated behind cloud services.
- • Custom Firmware & Hacking: Popular among hobbyists and embedded developers because it runs Linux and can be accessed via TTL serial or flashed with custom firmware like OpenIPC.
