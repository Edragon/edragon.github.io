


# HY2113-dat

- [[battery-protector-1s-dat]] - [[HY2113-dat]] - [[HYCONtek-dat]] - [[ZXW602-dat]]

The marking **301B** (or 3O1B) on a SOT23-6 package commonly corresponds to the HY2113-OB1B, which is a single-cell lithium-ion / lithium-polymer battery protection IC (specifically an overcharge, over-discharge, and overcurrent protection IC manufactured by HXY/HYCON or similar low-power analog suppliers).

Key Characteristics (HY2113-OB1B / 301B):

• Package Type: SOT23-6 (6-lead surface-mount package)
• Function: Single-cell Li-ion/Li-Po battery protection (overcharge detection, over-discharge detection, and load/short-circuit current protection)
• Common Marking: **301B** or **3O1B**
