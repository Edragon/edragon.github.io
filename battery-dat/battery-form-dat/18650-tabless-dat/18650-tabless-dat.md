### EVE INR18650-30PL


The **EVE INR18650-30PL** is a next-generation, high-performance, high-drain lithium-ion battery cell manufactured by EVE Energy. It is widely considered one of the most powerful 18650 cells available due to its unique internal architecture and massive current handling capabilities.

#### Key Specifications & Features

* **Form Factor:** 18650 (Standard cylindrical: ~18.35 mm diameter x 65.15 mm height)
* **Capacity:** 3000 mAh (Nominal/Minimum)
* **Nominal Voltage:** 3.65V
* **Discharge Rating:** Rated up to **60A** maximum discharge (with a recommended thermal cutoff of 75°C–80°C). Independent testing generally places its realistic continuous work threshold around 45A, which is exceptionally high for an 18650 size cell.
* **Internal Resistance (IR):** Extremely low (typically $\le 3.5\text{ m}\Omega$ to $6\text{ m}\Omega$), which minimizes voltage sag under heavy loads.

#### What Does "Tabless" Mean?

In a standard cylindrical battery cell, current must flow through a thin metallic "tab" welded to the ends of the jellyroll to connect it to the terminals. This creates a bottleneck, causing higher internal resistance, heat buildup, and voltage sag under high currents.

A **tabless design** removes this single-point bottleneck by making the entire edge of the cathode and anode foils act as a collective current collector. This structural shift allows current to flow across thousands of paths simultaneously rather than a single narrow strip.

#### Why It Matters (Pros & Cons)

* **Massive Power Output & Low Heat:** Because of the tabless architecture, the 30PL runs significantly cooler and maintains a higher voltage under intense loads compared to standard tabbed cells.
* **Ideal Applications:** High-drain devices where voltage stability and low heat generation are critical, such as high-performance e-bikes, power tools, high-end flashlights, drones, RC/UAV builds, and custom high-current battery packs.
* **Trade-offs:** While it excels at high-current output and voltage retention, applications requiring maximum run-time over raw power output often benefit more from standard high-capacity cells optimized for energy density rather than high discharge rates.
