


# 25x128-dat


- [[gigadevice-dat]] - 25Q128ESIG EFL287 - [[flash-nor-dat]] - [[25x128-dat]]


The `25Q128ESIG` (commonly marked as GD25Q128ESIG or GD25Q128ESIGR for tape-and-reel) is a 128 Mbit (16 MB) serial NOR flash memory IC made by GigaDevice.

Key Specifications

- • Capacity: 128 Mbit / 16 MByte (16M × 8 organization)
- • Interface: Standard, Dual, and Quad SPI (up to 133 MHz clock frequency)
- • Supply Voltage: 2.7 V to 3.6 V
- • Package: 8-pin SOP (SOP-8 / 8-SOIC, surface mount)
- • Operating Temperature: -40°C to +85°C
- • Memory Structure: 4 KB uniform sectors, 32 KB/64 KB uniform blocks, 256-byte programmable pages, 128-bit Unique ID, and 3 × 1024-byte OTP security registers
- • Endurance & Retention: 100,000 program/erase cycles and ~20-year data retention
