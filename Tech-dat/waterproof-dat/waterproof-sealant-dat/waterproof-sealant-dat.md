## potting 


### potting LED

Protecting only the LED leads with waterproof sealant (e.g., common conformal coating, silicone rubber, epoxy resin) **is usually NOT enough for direct, long-term submersion in water**.

Whether an LED can be submerged depends mainly on the **coverage of the waterproof sealant** and the **sealing process**:

### 1. Why sealing only the leads is "not safe" for submersion?

* **Incomplete encapsulation**: If only the leads are spot-sealed, water vapor can easily penetrate through tiny gaps and poorly bonded areas between the adhesive and the metal leads (at the junction of the LED die, molding compound, and leads).
* **Capillary action & water pressure**: Once submerged (especially at some depth), water seeps along the lead gaps via capillary action and water pressure, reaching the internal circuitry and causing short circuits, electrode corrosion (electrochemical migration), or burnout.
* **Water vapor permeation (moisture transmission rate)**: Ordinary adhesives (e.g., standard epoxy resin or hot-melt glue) are not absolutely waterproof. Under prolonged water pressure or immersion, water molecules slowly diffuse through the adhesive layer.

### 2. What kind of waterproofing allows submersion?

- [[silicone-dat]] - [[polyurethane-dat]] - [[epoxy-resin-dat]] == 透明硅胶、聚氨酯（PU）胶或环氧树脂

For an LED to be safely submerged (e.g., in fish tanks, water features, underwater lights, etc.), the following standards must be met:

* **Full encapsulation / potting**: Do not just seal the leads — use professional **transparent silicone, polyurethane (PU), or epoxy resin** to fully pot or extrude-coat the entire LED and solder joints (achieving an **IP68** waterproof rating).
* **Bubble-free and fully bonded**: The adhesive must bond seamlessly with the LED surface and wires, with no bubbles or delamination.

### 3. Practical recommendations

* **If only splash-proofing, moisture resistance, or occasional rain exposure**: Carefully applying waterproof sealant (e.g., silicone rubber, conformal coating) to protect the leads and solder pads is sufficient.
* **If long-term or permanent submersion**: Spot-sealing only the leads is **very dangerous** — it easily causes leakage or damage. You must use professional **IP68-rated waterproof fixtures** or fully pot them using a mold. Additionally, when mains voltage is involved, always use **safe low voltage (e.g., 12V or 24V)** for underwater use to ensure personal safety.
