


# openIPC-use-dat


How to Connect and Use OpenIPC on SSC338 After Flashing

Once you have successfully flashed the OpenIPC FPV firmware onto your SSC338 module, connecting and using it involves setting up the physical links, accessing the management interface, and integrating it with your FPV setup (such as Ruby FPV or OpenHD).



## 1. Physical Connections

Ensure your camera module is correctly wired to your aircraft components:

* **Power Supply:** Connect a clean, stable **5V power supply** (via a BEC from your flight battery) to the camera board.
* **Telemetry / Flight Controller:** Connect the camera's UART TX/RX pins to an available telemetry UART on your Flight Controller (e.g., ArduPilot, Betaflight, or INav) to pass MAVLink or MSP data.
* **Network / Radio Link:**
  * For digital FPV systems, plug your supported USB Wi-Fi adapter (or internal Wi-Fi card, depending on your board layout) into the camera's USB port.
  * Alternatively, connect via an Ethernet adapter or Wi-Fi router for initial desktop bench testing.




## 2. Initial Access & Web Interface (Majestic UI)

1. **Power on** the camera module.
2. Connect your PC to the same network (or connect directly via an Ethernet cable/router if your build uses one).
3. Find the camera's IP address (you can check your router's client list or view it via the UART serial terminal).
4. Open a web browser and enter: `http://`
5. **Login Credentials**:
* **Username:** `root`
* **Password:** `12345` (or whatever was set during your firmware build)
6. Inside the **Majestic Web UI**, you can adjust video encoding settings (bitrate, resolution, codec like H.264/H.265), sensor parameters, and frame rates.


## 3. Connecting to Your FPV Ground Station (Ruby FPV / OpenHD)

To pair your newly flashed SSC338 air unit with your ground station controller:

1. **SSH into the Camera:** Use a tool like PuTTY or terminal (`ssh root@`).
2. **Configure Pairing Keys:** Place your matched encryption/pairing key files (generated by your FPV system config tool) into the appropriate folder on the camera (usually `/etc/` or the path specified by OpenHD/Ruby documentation) so it can handshake securely with your ground station receiver.
3. **Configure Telemetry:** Ensure the telemetry stream is enabled in the OpenIPC configuration to forward flight controller data (MAVLink/MSP) to the wireless transmission link.

## 4. Troubleshooting & Verification

* **Check Video Feed:** Boot up your ground station; if keys and Wi-Fi channels match, the high-definition video feed from the SSC338 should appear automatically with your OSD overlay.
* **Serial Debugging:** If something goes wrong, plug your USB-to-TTL adapter back into the UART port to inspect boot logs and service statuses in real-time.
