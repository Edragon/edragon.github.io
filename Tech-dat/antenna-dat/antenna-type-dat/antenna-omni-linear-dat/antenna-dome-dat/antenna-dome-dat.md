## for 2.4ghz 

Low-Profile "Puck" / Dome Antenna (Screw-Mount or Adhesive)
* **What it is:** A small, flat, disk-shaped or low-profile plastic housing (resembling a small hockey puck or a mushroom cap) that mounts externally on top or on the side of an enclosure, secured by a threaded bolt and nut with an internal cable.
* **Why it's better:** 
  * It sits flush against the device body, making it extremely durable, tamper-proof, and aesthetically clean.
  * It offers great omnidirectional coverage while being much harder to accidentally snap off compared to a sticking-out rod.
* **Best for:** Small outdoor/indoor enclosures, IoT gateways, or devices that need to look professional and rugged.
