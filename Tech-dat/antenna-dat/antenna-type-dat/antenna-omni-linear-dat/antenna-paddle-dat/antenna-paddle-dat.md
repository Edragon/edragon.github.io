


# antenna-paddle-dat




## for 2.4ghz 

articulating Right-Angle "Paddle" / Fin Antenna
* **What it is:** A flat, paddle-shaped external antenna (often used on modern FPV goggles, high-end routers, or drones) that features a 90-degree hinge (SMA connector).
* **Why it's better:** 
  * It has a very slim profile when folded flat against the device, but provides better gain and efficiency than a stubby antenna.
  * You can pivot and adjust its angle to optimize signal reception.
