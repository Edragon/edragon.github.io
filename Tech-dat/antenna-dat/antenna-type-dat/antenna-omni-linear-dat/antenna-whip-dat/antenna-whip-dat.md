## for 2.4ghz wifi device 


1. Miniature "Stubby" / Short Rubber Duck Antenna (Short Whip)

* **What it is:** A tiny, rigid external antenna (usually only **20mm to 30mm** in length) enclosed in a hard plastic or rubber shell, with an SMA or RP-SMA connector directly on the base.
* **Why it's better:** 
  * It gives you the high efficiency and robust performance of an external metal-element antenna, but without the long, annoying "wand" look of standard router antennas.
  * It maintains a clean 360-degree omnidirectional and linear-polarized pattern.
* **Best for:** Compact routers, mini-PCs, telemetry boxes, or custom wireless nodes where you want external performance without a large footprint.
