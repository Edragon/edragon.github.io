- [[vishay-dat]] - [[mosfet-dat]] - [[vishay-mosfet-dat]]

The `7149A` marking on power MOSFETs commonly refers to the Vishay Siliconix `SI7149` series (such as the SI7149DP-T1-GE3 or SI7149ADP).

Key Specifications

• Type: P-Channel (D-S) MOSFET
• Technology: TrenchFET® Power MOSFET
• Drain-Source Voltage (\(V_{DS}\)): -30 V
• Gate-Source Voltage (\(V_{GS}\)): ±25 V
• Continuous Drain Current (\(I_{D}\)): -50 A (package limited) / -23.7 A (\(T_A = 25^\circ\text{C}\))
• On-Resistance (\(R_{DS(on)}\)):
	• Max 0.0052 Ω at \(V_{GS} = -10\text{ V}\)
	• Max 0.0094 Ω at \(V_{GS} = -4.5\text{ V}\)
• Package: PowerPAK® SO-8 (Surface Mount)
• Operating Temperature: -55°C to 150°C


The `R158` marking typically refers to the Vishay `SiR158DP` (such as part number SIR158DP-T1-RE3 or SIR158DP-T1-GE3), an N-channel power MOSFET housed in a PowerPAK® SO-8 package.

Key Specifications

• Transistor Polarity: N-Channel
• Drain-Source Voltage (\(V_{DS}\)): 30 V
• Gate-Source Voltage (\(V_{GS}\)): ±20 V
• Continuous Drain Current (\(I_{D}\)): Up to 60 A – 65 A (package/thermal limited)
• On-Resistance (\(R_{DS(on)}\)):
	• ~0.00145 Ω (1.45 mΩ) typ. at \(V_{GS} = 10\text{ V}\)
	• ~0.00185 Ω (1.85 mΩ) typ. at \(V_{GS} = 4.5\text{ V}\)
• Power Dissipation (\(P_{D}\)): 83 W
• Package Type: PowerPAK SO-8 (single)
