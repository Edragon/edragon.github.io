### NCE4953

The `NCE4953` is a dual P-Channel enhancement mode power MOSFET manufactured by Wuxi NCE Power Semiconductor Co., Ltd, typically housed in an SOP-8 (SO-8) package.

Key Specifications

• Type: Dual P-Channel Enhancement Mode Trench MOSFET
• Package: SOP-8 / SO-8
• Drain-Source Voltage (\(V_{DS}\)): -30 V
• Gate-Source Voltage (\(V_{GS}\)): ±20 V
• Continuous Drain Current (\(I_{D}\)): -5.1 A to -7.3 A (depending on conditions/mounting)
• On-Resistance (\(R_{DS(on)}\)): ~0.029 – 0.035 Ω typ. at \(V_{GS} = -10\,\text{V}\)
• Total Gate Charge (\(Q_{g}\)): ~17 nC typ.
• Operating Temperature (\(T_{j}\)): -55°C to +150°C

Common Applications

• Load switches
• Pulse-Width Modulation (PWM) applications
• Power management circuits
