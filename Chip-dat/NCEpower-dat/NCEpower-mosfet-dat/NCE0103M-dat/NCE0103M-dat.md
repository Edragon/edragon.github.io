


# NCE0103M-dat

0103M SOT89 - [[NCEpower-mosfet-dat]] - [[NCE0103M-dat]] - [[mosfet-dat]] - [[fiber-media-converter-dat]]


The 0103M marking in a SOT-89 package most commonly identifies the NCE0103M, an N-channel enhancement mode power MOSFET made by Wuxi NCE Power Semiconductor. First Silicon Co., Ltd. also uses the 0103M marking for the FTK0103M in an SOT-89-3L package.

Key Specifications (NCE0103M)

- • Package: SOT-89-3L surface-mount
- • Drain-Source Voltage (\(V_{DS}\)): 100 V
- • Continuous Drain Current (\(I_{D}\)): 3 A (pulsed \(I_{DM}\) up to 20 A)
- • Gate-Source Voltage (\(V_{GS}\)): ±20 V
- • On-State Resistance (\(R_{DS(ON)}\)): < 160 mΩ at \(V_{GS} = 10\text{ V}\) (typical 136 mΩ); < 170 mΩ at \(V_{GS} = 4.5\text{ V}\) (typical 140 mΩ)
- • Max Power Dissipation (\(P_{D}\)): 1.5 W
- • Operating Junction/Storage Temp: -55°C to 150°C
