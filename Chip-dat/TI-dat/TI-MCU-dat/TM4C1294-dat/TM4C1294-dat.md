The Texas Instruments TM4C1294 is a 32-bit ARM Cortex-M4F-based microcontroller belonging to the Tiva™ C Series. It is widely used for connected embedded systems, internet-of-things (IoT) applications, and real-time control.

Key Specifications

• Core: ARM Cortex-M4F processor with FPU (Floating-Point Unit) running up to 120 MHz
• Memory: 1 MB Flash memory, 256 KB SRAM, and 6 KB EEPROM
• Connectivity: Integrated 10/100 Ethernet MAC and PHY, USB 2.0 (Host/Device/OTG), CAN, UART, QSSI/SPI, and I²C
• Peripherals: Dual 12-bit 2 MSPS ADCs, motion-control PWMs, quadrature encoder inputs (QEI), analog comparators, and multiple 32-bit timers
• Package: Typically available in a 128-pin TQFP or 212-pin NFBGA package
