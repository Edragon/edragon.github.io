


# SAM2195-dat


- [[SAM2195-dat]] - [[SAM2695-dat]] - [[dream-dat]] 

- [[audio-dat]] - [[MIDI-dat]] - [[SYNTHESIZER-dat]]



The SAM2195 Data Sheet is a single-chip, low-power MIDI synthesizer and effects processor manufactured in a QFN44 package, originally associated with Dream / Atmel designs and famously used in modules like the DreamBlaster S1 wavetable daughterboard.

Key Specifications

- Polyphony: 64-voice polyphony (dry / no effects) or 38-voice polyphony with effects enabled.
- Audio & Effects: Built-in General MIDI sound set, reverb, chorus, 4-band equalizer, spatial effects, and a stereo DAC.
- Power & Supply: ~75mW typical operation, under 1µA in power-down mode, operating on a single 3.3V or 1.8V supply.
- Package: QFN44 (7 x 7 mm) surface-mount chip.
- Tweakability: Supports extensive real-time adjustments via NRPN (Non-Registered Parameter Number) and SysEx messages (such as toggling effects, master volume/pan, and hard/soft clipping modes).
