The Ralink RT3072 Wireless Adapter is a legacy Wi-Fi 4 (IEEE 802.11n) USB network card featuring a 2T2R MIMO configuration with speeds up to 300Mbps on the 2.4 GHz band.

Key Specifications

• Chipset: Ralink / MediaTek RT3072 (Vendor/Device ID: 148F:3072)
• Standard: IEEE 802.11n, 802.11g, 802.11b
• Speed: Up to 300 Mbps (2T2R MIMO)
• Frequency: 2.4 GHz
• Security: 64/128-bit WEP, WPA, WPA2, WPA-PSK, WPA2-PSK, and WPS
