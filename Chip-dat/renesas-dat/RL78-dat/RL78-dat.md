R7F124FGJ3AFB-C#AA0 is a 16-bit microcontroller from `Renesas` Electronics in the `RL78`/F24 family, designed for automotive actuator and sensor applications.

Key Specifications (RL78/F24 Series)

• Core & Speed: 16-bit RL78-S3 core up to 40 MHz
• Memory: 256 KB program flash, 16 KB data flash, and 24 KB RAM with ECC
• Package: 48-lead LFQFP (7 × 7 mm)
• Operating Range: 2.7 V to 5.5 V supply; -40°C to +105°C ambient temperature
• Peripherals & Interfaces: 38 I/O pins, 19-channel 12-bit ADC, 8-bit DAC, analog comparator, CAN, CAN-FD, LIN, UART, SPI, I2C, and timers/PWM
• Security: Secure boot, AES, TRNG, and Evita-Light security features
