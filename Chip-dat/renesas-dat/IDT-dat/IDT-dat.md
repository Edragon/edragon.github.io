


# IDT-dat

- [[latch-dat]] - [[IDT-dat]] - [[logic-dat]] - [[54FCT573TDB-dat]] - [[renesas-dat]]

The 54FCT573TDB (originally IDT, now Renesas Electronics) is a non-inverting octal transparent D-type latch with 3-state outputs in a 20-pin CERDIP (CDIP-20) package, designed for bus-oriented applications.

Key Specifications & Features

• Logic Function: `Octal transparent latch` (8-bit, 1 channel) with 3-state outputs.
• Control Behavior: Outputs follow data inputs transparently when Latch Enable (LE) is high and hold latched data when LE goes low. Data appears on the bus when Output Enable (OE) is low and enters high-impedance when OE is high.
• Supply & I/O Compatibility: Operates at 5V with true TTL input/output compatibility (VOH = 3.3V typical, VOL = 0.3V typical).
• Output Drive & Leakage: -15 mA IOH, 48 mA IOL, and ≤ 1 µA maximum input/output leakage current.
• Package & Standards: 20-pin CERDIP (ceramic dual in-line package) built to military/high-reliability standards (MIL-STD-883 Class B / DESC compliant) with power-off disable outputs for live insertion.
• Lifecycle Status: Marked as obsolete by distributors such as Mouser; see the 54/74FCT573T Datasheet for full electrical and timing details.
