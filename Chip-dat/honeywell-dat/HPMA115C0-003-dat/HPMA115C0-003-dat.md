


# HPMA115C0-003-dat


The Honeywell HPMA115C0-003 is a compact, laser-based particulate matter (PM) sensor module in the HPM Series that measures PM1.0, PM2.5, PM4.0, and PM10 concentrations using light scattering.

Key Specifications & Status

- • Lifecycle Status: Obsolete / End-of-Life (EOL date: October 1, 2025, per DigiKey).
- • Measurement Range: 0 to 1,000 µg/m³.
- • Interface & Power: UART output, 5V supply.
- • Response Time: Under 6 seconds.
- • Operating Temperature: -20°C to 70°C.
- • Physical Design: Compact housing with air intake and exhaust on the same side.
