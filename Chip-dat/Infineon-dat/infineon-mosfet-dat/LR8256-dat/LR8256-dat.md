- [[LR8256-dat]] - [[mosfet-dat]] - [[IOR-dat]] - [[infineon-dat]] - [[ESC-dat]] - [[infineon-mosfet-dat]]


The LR8256 (commonly labeled as IRLR8256) is a surface-mount N-channel power MOSFET packaged in a standard DPAK (TO-252). It handles up to 25V with a low on-resistance of around 5.7 mΩ and high current capability, making it ideal for high-frequency DC-DC converters and synchronous buck applications.

https://www.infineon.com/assets/row/public/documents/24/49/infineon-irlr8256-datasheet-en.pdf


## Key Specifications

- Type: N-Channel
- Package: DPAK / TO-252
- Drain-Source Voltage (\(V_{DS}\)): 25 V
- Continuous Drain Current (\(I_{D}\)): Up to 81 A
- On-Resistance (\(R_{DS(on)}\)): ~5.7 mΩ
- Max Operating Temperature: 175°C
