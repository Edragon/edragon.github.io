


# panchip-dat

- [[panchip-dat]] - [[XN297-dat]]

The XN297 (and its variant XN297L) is a single-chip, low-power 2.4 GHz RF transceiver designed by Panchip Microelectronics using GFSK modulation.

Key Specifications & Features

• Frequency Band: 2.400 – 2.483 GHz (ISM band)
• Data Rates: Supports 250 kbps, 1 Mbps, and 2 to 2 Mbps
• Modulation: GFSK / FSK
• Operating Voltage: 2.0V – 3.6V (or 2.3V–3.3V for XN297L)
• Interface: SPI control interface
• Communication Modes: Normal BURST (no ACK) and Enhanced BURST (auto ACK/retransmission) with dual 32-byte or single 64-byte TX/RX FIFOs and up to 6 receive data pipes
• Packages: Available in SOP8, SOP16, and QFN20 (3x3 mm)
