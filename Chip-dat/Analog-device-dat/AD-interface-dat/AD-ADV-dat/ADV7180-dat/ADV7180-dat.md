


# ADV7180-dat


- [[SDTV-dat]] - [[TV-dat]] - [[CCTV-dat]] - [[video-dat]] - [[AD-ADV-dat]]

The ADV7180BSTZ is a 10-bit, 4x oversampling standard-definition television (SDTV) video decoder IC manufactured by Analog Devices Inc.

	
Video Decoder IC I2C, Serial ITU-R BT.656, NTSC, PAL, SECAM 64-LQFP (10x10) Package

The ADV7180 automatically detects and converts standard analog baseband television signals compatible with worldwide NTSC, PAL, and SECAM standards into 4:2:2 component video data compatible with the 8-bit ITU-R BT.656 interface standard.
