


# JW5250-dat

JWM3L - [[joulwatt-dat]] - [[JW5250-dat]] == 6V 1A - [[dcdc-down-dat]]

JWM3L is the surface-mount marking code for the JW5250A, a synchronous step-down (buck) DC-DC converter made by JoulWatt Technology.

Key Specifications (JW5250A)

- • Package: SOT23-5
- • Input Voltage: 2.7V to 6.0V (UVLO rising ~2.5V typical)
- • Output Current: Up to 1A continuous
- • Feedback Voltage: 0.6V (adjustable output down to 0.6V via resistor divider)
- • Switching Frequency: 1.5 MHz (constant on-time control)
- • Quiescent Current: ~40 μA typical; <1 μA in shutdown
- • Protections: Input under-voltage lockout (UVLO), hot-plug in over-voltage protection (~6.6V typical), short-circuit/FB short protection, and thermal shutdown (150°C)
