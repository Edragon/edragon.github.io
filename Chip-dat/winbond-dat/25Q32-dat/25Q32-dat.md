



# 25Q32-dat


- [[25Q32-dat]] - [[flash-nor-dat]] - [[winbond-dat]]

The Winbond 25Q32 (such as the W25Q32JV and W25Q32BV) is a 32-Mbit (4 MB) Serial NOR Flash memory chip with Standard, Dual, and Quad SPI interfaces. It is commonly used for PC BIOS/UEFI firmware, routers, TVs, and embedded code/data storage.

Key Specifications

- • Capacity: 32 Mbit (4M × 8-bit)
- • Memory Organization: 16,384 pages of 256 bytes each; erasable in 4 KB sectors, 32 KB/64 KB blocks, or full chip
- • Interface: Standard, Dual, and Quad SPI (supports Execute-in-Place / XIP and code shadowing)
- • Clock Speed: Up to 133 MHz on newer revisions like W25Q32JV (earlier 25Q32 revisions rated at 80 MHz)
- • Supply Voltage: 2.7 V to 3.6 V
- • Power Consumption: ~1 µA typical in power-down mode
- • Endurance & Retention: Typically 100,000 erase/write cycles and 20-year data retention
- • Common Packages: 8-pin SOIC (150-mil or 208/209-mil), 8-pin WSON, 8-pin PDIP, and 16-pin SOIC
