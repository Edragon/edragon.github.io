The `ITA15N50A` is an N-Channel Power MOSFET manufactured by IPS (InnoPower Semiconductor / Integrated Power Semiconductors).


Key Specifications & Ratings

• Part Number: ITA15N50A (related to ITP15N50A)
• Transistor Type: N-Channel MOSFET
• Package Type: TO-220 / TO-220F
• Drain-Source Voltage (\(V_{DSS}\)): 500 V
• Continuous Drain Current (\(I_{D}\)): 15 A
• On-Resistance (\(R_{DS(on)}\)): Typ. 0.33 Ω
• Gate-Source Voltage (\(V_{GS}\)): ±30 V
• Total Power Dissipation (\(P_{D}\)): 140 W / 60 W
• Single Pulse Avalanche Energy (EAS): 1000 mJ
