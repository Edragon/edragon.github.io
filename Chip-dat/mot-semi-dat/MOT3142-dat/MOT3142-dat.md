



# MOT3142-dat

- [[mot-semi-dat]] - [[MOT3142-dat]] - [[mosfet-dat]] - [[PDFN-dat]] == 30V / 60A


The MOT3142J is an N-channel trench MOSFET manufactured by MOT Semiconductor, housed in a compact PDFN3×3 package and rated for 30 V and 60 A.

It is designed for low-loss switching in motor-control circuits, battery management systems (BMS), PWM control, load switching, and DC power management.

Key specifications

According to the MOT Semiconductor MOT3142J product page, the core electrical ratings are:
• Polarity. N-Channel Trench SGT MOSFET
• Package. PDFN3×3 (3 mm × 3 mm)
• Drain-to-source voltage (\(BV_{DSS}\)). 30 V
• Continuous drain current (\(I_{D}\) at 25 °C). 60 A
• On-resistance (\(R_{DS(on)}\) at \(V_{GS} = 10\text{ V}\)). 5 mΩ (typical)
• Gate threshold voltage (\(V_{GS(th)}\) max). 1 V
