


# LM323-dat


- [[lmxx-dat]] - [[LM323-dat]] - [[LDO-dat]] == 5V/3A


The LM323 is a three-terminal linear positive voltage regulator that delivers a fixed 5V output at up to 3A of load current.

3A 5V Fixed Output Linear Regulator


Key Specifications

• Output Voltage: Fixed +5V
• Output Current: Up to 3A
• Input Voltage: 7.5V minimum; typically operates from 7.5V to 15V (20V maximum)
• Power Dissipation: Up to 30W (with adequate heat sinking)
• Protection Features: Internal current limiting, power limiting, and thermal shutdown
• Operating Junction Temperature: 0°C to +125°C for standard LM323/LM323-N (-40°C to +125°C for LM323A)
• Package: Typically housed in a metal TO-3 package (such as the LM323K) for higher thermal dissipation
