The TDA7498 is a 100W + 100W dual Bridge-Tied Load (BTL) Class-D audio amplifier IC made by STMicroelectronics for home audio systems and active speakers.

Key Specifications

• Output Power (THD = 10%): 100W + 100W into 6Ω at 36V \(V_{CC}\); 80W + 80W into 8Ω at 34V \(V_{CC}\)
• Power Supply: Single supply, 14V–39V operating range (45V absolute maximum)
• Efficiency: Up to ~90% Class-D operation
• Gain Settings: Four selectable fixed gains—25.6 dB, 31.6 dB, 35.1 dB, and 37.6 dB
• Package: 36-pin PowerSSO with exposed pad up (EPU) for heatsink mounting
• Protection & Controls: Standby and mute modes, differential inputs, external clock sync, and over-voltage, under-voltage, over-current, and thermal protection
