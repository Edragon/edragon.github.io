


# azureware-dat

- [[azureware-dat]] - [[AW-NM372SM-dat]] - [[wifi-dat]] - [[bluetooth-dat]] - [[bobcat-dat]]

The AzureWave AW-NM372SM is a compact wireless combo module developed by AzureWave Technologies.

Key Specifications & Features

• Manufacturer: AzureWave Technologies, Inc. (FCC ID: TLZ-NM372SM)
• Wireless Standards: IEEE 802.11 b/g/n WLAN, Bluetooth, and FM combo support
• Data Rate: Up to 150 Mbps
• Frequency Range: 2402–2480 MHz (Bluetooth/WLAN 2.4 GHz)
• Interface: SDIO module
• Package / Size: Surface-mount device (SMD), approximately 12 x 12 mm
• Target Applications: Mobile, embedded, and IoT devices requiring low power consumption and small footprint integration.
