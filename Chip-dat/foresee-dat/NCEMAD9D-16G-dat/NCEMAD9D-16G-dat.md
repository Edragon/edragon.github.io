



# NCEMAD9D-16G-dat

- [[foresee-dat]] - [[NCEMAD9D-16G-dat]] - [[EMMC-dat]] 



The FORESEE NCEMAD9D-16G is a 16GB eMMC 5.1 embedded flash memory chip in a 153-ball FBGA package (11.5 × 13 × 1.0 mm).

Key Specifications

• Standard: JEDEC eMMC 5.1 (backward compatible with 4.41, 4.5, and 5.0)
• Capacity: 16GB
• Package: FBGA-153 (11.5 × 13 × 1.0 mm), RoHS compliant
• Bus Interface: 1-bit, 4-bit, or 8-bit data bus; HS400 and HS200 modes up to 400 MB/s transfer rate; clock frequency 0–200 MHz
• Sequential Performance: Read up to 240 MB/s; write up to 90 MB/s (x8 bus, 200 MHz DDR)
• Supply Voltage: Vcc (NAND) 2.7–3.6V; Vccq (Controller) 1.7–1.95V or 2.7–3.6V
• Temperature Range: Operating −25°C to +85°C; storage −40°C to +85°C
• Built-in Features: Hardware ECC, global wear leveling, sudden-power-loss safeguard, Initial Data Acceleration (IDA, up to 4.8GB limit), S.M.A.R.T. health reporting, FFU, RPMB (4MB), boot partitions (4096KB), trim/discard/sanitize, and background operations
