The `77546-11` (Skyworks SKY77546) is a transmit and receive Front-End Module (FEM) manufactured by Skyworks Solutions.

Key Specifications & Function

- • Type: RF Front-End Module (FEM) / Power Amplifier and Signal Control IC
- • Supported Bands: Designed for dual-band cellular handsets operating on GSM900 and DCS1800.
- • Capabilities: Supports Class 12 General Packet Radio Service (GPRS) multi-slot operations and EDGE modulation.
- • Internal Components: Integrates GSM900 and DCS1800 PA blocks, 50-ohm impedance matching circuitry, TX harmonics filtering, high-linearity RF switches, and a custom CMOS integrated circuit for Power Amplifier Control (PAC).
- • Application: Used in mobile phone motherboards/PCBs to handle cellular signal transmission and reception
