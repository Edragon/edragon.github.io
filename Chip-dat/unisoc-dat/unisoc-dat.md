The Spreadtrum SL8521ET (now branded under UNISOC) is a sub-variant of the SL8521E system-on-chip (SoC) designed primarily for low-power 4G smartwatches, IoT devices, and entry-level mobile hardware. The "T" suffix typically designates a specific regional modem configuration, packaging revision, or temperature-grade bin derived from the standard SL8521E silicon.

Core Specifications (SL8521E Platform)

The `SL8521ET` shares its underlying architecture with the standard UNISOC 8521E, which is built on a 28nm process to balance battery efficiency with 4G connectivity.


- • CPU Architecture. Features a dual-core Arm Cortex-A53 processor clocked up to 1.3 GHz or 1.4 GHz (some early smartwatch implementations also utilize Cortex-A7 configurations at 1.2 GHz).
- • Graphics (GPU). Integrates an Arm Mali-T820 MP1 GPU running at up to 600 MHz for basic user interfaces and 720p video playback at 30fps.
- • Cellular Modem. Includes an integrated 2G/3G/4G LTE Cat.4 modem supporting FDD-LTE, TDD-LTE, WCDMA, and GSM bands, along with VoLTE for voice and video calls.
- • Wireless & Navigation. Combines Wi-Fi 4 (802.11 b/g/n at 2.4 GHz), Bluetooth 4.2, FM radio, and multi-constellation GNSS (GPS, GLONASS, and BeiDou) on a single chip.
- • Memory & Camera Support. Supports LPDDR2/LPDDR3 memory (commonly paired with 512MB RAM + 4GB ROM in wearables) and camera sensors up to 5 megapixels.
