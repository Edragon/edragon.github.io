- [[SR3593A-dat]] - [[RF-transceiver-dat]] - [[SPRD-dat]] - [[UNISOC-dat]]


The SR3593A (often marked SPRD SR3593A) is a mobile Radio Frequency (RF) transceiver integrated circuit (IC) manufactured by Spreadtrum (now UNISOC).

The "SPRD" marking on the chip package is the standard manufacturer abbreviation for Spreadtrum, though it is frequently misread or typed as "SPDR."

Key Details

• Manufacturer. Spreadtrum Communications (rebranded as UNISOC).
• Component Type. Mobile RF / Intermediate Frequency (IF) Transceiver IC.
• Function. Handles cellular radio frequency signal transmission and reception on smartphone and feature phone motherboards.
• Package Style. Surface-mount Ball Grid Array (BGA) chip.
• Related Variants. Part of the SR3593 family, which includes the SR3593D and SR3593L.

Common Contexts

• Mobile Phone Repair. The SR3593A is commonly encountered as a replacement part on smartphones powered by UNISOC/Spreadtrum chipsets. A faulty SR3593A typically causes "No Service," weak cellular reception, or baseband/network drop issues, requiring micro-soldering and BGA reballing to replace.
• Semiconductor Analysis. Technical teardown firms such as TechInsights have published architectural and die floorplan reports analyzing the SR3593A's internal RF circuitry.
