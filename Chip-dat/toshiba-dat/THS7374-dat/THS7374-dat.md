

# THS7374-dat

The THS7374 is a `4-channel standard-definition television (SDTV)` video amplifier with integrated 6th-order low-pass filters and a fixed 6-dB gain, manufactured by Texas Instruments. It is widely used in `retro gaming modifications` (such as RGB bypass boards for N64 and SNES consoles) to clean up and sharpen analog video signals
