Installation:

Copy ELRS_Finder.lua into /SCRIPTS/TOOLS/ on your SD card.

On your radio: Long-press SYS → Tools tab → run ELRS Finder
For best results:
- Set fixed low TX power (10–25 mW) in ELRS menu
- Sweep the radio slowly to locate signal peaks
