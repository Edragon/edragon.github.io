## What is the JR Module Interface?

The **JR Interface** (commonly referred to as the **JR Bay** or **Micro Module Bay**) is the most traditional and widely used standard physical size and connector format for external radio frequency (RF) modules in RC modeling and FPV drones.

The name comes from **JR Propo**, an iconic Japanese RC brand. JR pioneered this standardized, removable module bay design on the back of their high-end radios decades ago. It was subsequently adopted across the entire hobby industry—by brands such as FrSky, FlySky, RadioMaster, and TBS—becoming the de facto industry standard for OpenTX and EdgeTX transmitters.

---

### 1. Key Characteristics of the JR Interface

* **Physical Dimensions (Full-Size / Micro Bay):** 
  The JR bay is larger, with module housing measuring roughly **44 mm wide × 63 mm tall**. By comparison, the **Nano bay** used on the RadioMaster Pocket measures only about **30 mm wide**.
* **Pinout (Typically 5-Pin):** 
  A standard JR bay features a 5-pin header inside the bay that supplies power, ground, and telemetry/control data (such as CRSF, PPM, S.PORT, or MULTI protocols).

---

### 2. RF Module Bay Sizes Compared

When buying an external RF module, you will encounter three main form factors. **You must match the module form factor to your transmitter's rear module bay:**

| Module Bay Name | Aliases / Common Names | Approximate Size | Representative Transmitters |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **JR Interface** | JR Bay, Micro Module Bay, Full-Size Bay | ~44mm × 63mm | RadioMaster TX16S, BOXER, FrSky Taranis X9D |
| **Nano Interface** | Lite Bay, Nano Bay, Small Bay | ~30mm × 52mm | **RadioMaster Pocket**, RadioMaster Zorro, TBS Tango 2 |
| **Slim Interface** | Slim Bay | Proprietary Slim | Select FrSky X-Lite series radios |

---

### 3. What If You Accidentally Buy a JR-Sized Module for a Pocket?

If you have a JR-sized (Micro) module and want to use it on a RadioMaster Pocket (Nano bay), you have two options:

1. **Use a Nano-to-JR 3D-Printed Adapter:**
   Commercial and 3D-printed adapter shells fit into the Pocket's Nano bay while providing a full-size JR slot on top, routing the 5-pin signals through an internal jumper wire.
2. **Swap the Module Housing:**
   Some modular transmitters (such as the official RadioMaster RM 4IN1 module) share the same internal circuit board across models. You can unscrew the module case and swap the JR outer shell for a Nano outer shell.
