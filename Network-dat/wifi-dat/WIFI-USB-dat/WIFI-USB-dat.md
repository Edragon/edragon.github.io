- [[Realtek-dat]] - [[RTL8812-dat]] - [[RTL8822-dat]]

If your SSC338 board has a dedicated USB port or USB pin header (often a 4-pin JST-GH or similar layout for D+, D-, GND, and 5V/3.3V), you must wire or plug a compatible external Wi-Fi/radio module (such as cards utilizing RTL8812AU, RTL8812EU, or RTL8822CU chipsets) into it.
