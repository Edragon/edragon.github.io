### What It Is

It is a tiny, chip-style passive RF component designed to split a single high-frequency signal into two paths (or combine two signals into one) while maintaining impedance matching. Because it handles RF frequencies, it is commonly used in high-frequency communication circuits, wireless modules, and test equipment.
