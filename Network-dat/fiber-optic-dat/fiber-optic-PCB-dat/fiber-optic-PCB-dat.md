If you want to integrate a paired **SM1310 + SM1510** (BiDi / single-fiber bidirectional) optical link onto your custom PCB, there are two ways to do it depending on whether you want a **pluggable socket** or a **directly soldered optical component**:


- [[fiber-optic-dat]] - [[BOSA-dat]] - [[fiber-optic-PCB-dat]]


* **For prototyping, custom dev boards, or easy maintenance:** Go with **Option 1 (SFP Cage + finished SM1310/SM1510 modules)**. It avoids complex analog laser-biasing circuits and lets you swap modules easily.
* **For ultra-compact size or high-volume cost reduction:** Go with **Option 2 (Direct-solder BOSA + driver ICs)**.
