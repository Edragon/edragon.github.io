


# SM1310-dat

- [[phy-dat]] - [[10GBASE-LR]] - [[1000BASE-LX]]

In networking hardware, SM1310 denotes a Single-Mode (SM) optical transceiver module operating at a 1310 nm wavelength. Most commonly seen on 1G (1000BASE-LX) SFP or 10G (10GBASE-LR) SFP+ modules, it supports distances up to 10 km over single-mode fiber (SMF) using an LC connector.

Typical Specifications (1G 1000BASE-LX SM1310)

• Wavelength: 1310 nm
• Fiber Type: Single-Mode Fiber (SMF) - [[optic-fiber-SMF-dat]] 
• Max Distance: Up to 10 km
• Connector: Duplex LC (or simplex LC on BiDi variants) - [[LC-connector-dat]]
• Data Rate: 1 Gbps / 1.25 Gbps (10 Gbps on 10GBASE-LR SM1310 models such as the Ruijie XG-SFP-LR-SM1310)
• Diagnostics: Digital Diagnostic Monitoring (DDM / DOM) on most models

Common Examples & Local Options (Singapore)

• Ruijie MINI-GBIC-LX-SM1310: 1000BASE-LX SFP module (1310 nm, 10 km, duplex LC, SMF, DDM). Available locally in Singapore through retailers such as Bizgram and Viskou.
• Huawei SFP-GE-LX-SM1310: 1G eSFP single-mode optical module (1310 nm, 10 km, LC, DDM).
• FS Compatible SFP-GE-LX-SM1310-D: 1000BASE-LX SFP transceiver (1310 nm, 10 km, duplex LC, SMF) listed on FS Singapore.
