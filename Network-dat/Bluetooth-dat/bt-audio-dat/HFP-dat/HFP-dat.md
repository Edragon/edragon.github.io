- [[HFP-dat]] - [[bt-audio-dat]] 



HFP (Hands-Free Profile): The Bluetooth protocol that handles two-way voice calls (like a car hands-free kit or wireless headset). Most cheap Bluetooth audio boards only support A2DP (one-way music streaming), so specifying HFP ensures the board can send and receive call audio at the same time.



In Bluetooth HFP terms, the phone acts as the Audio Gateway (AG) and the dev board acts as the Hands-Free Unit (HF). When a call comes in on the phone, the audio routes wirelessly to and from the board.
