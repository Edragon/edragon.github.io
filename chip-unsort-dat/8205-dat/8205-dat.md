## 8205HA 

- [[SW6005-dat]] - [[SW6808-dat]]  - [[power-bank-dat]] - [[ismartware-dat]]

The 8205HA (commonly referred to alongside the 8205 series like the 8205A) is a dual N-channel enhancement-mode power MOSFET designed primarily for compact low-voltage switching and lithium-ion (Li-ion) battery protection boards (BMS).

Key Specifications & Features

• Package Type: SOT-23-6 (surface mount)
• Configuration: Dual N-channel with common drains
• Drain-to-Source Voltage (\(V_{DS}\)): 20 V
• Continuous Drain Current (\(I_{D}\)): ~5 A to 6 A (at 25°C)
• Low On-Resistance (\(R_{DS(on)}\)): Typically around 18 mΩ to 27 mΩ (depending on gate-source voltage \(V_{GS}\))
• Common Applications: Single-cell or multi-cell Li-ion battery protection circuits, portable electronics, load switches, and DC-DC converters.
