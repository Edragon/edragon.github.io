1. **Technic Pin Hole (Pin Hole / Bearing Hole)**

- **Hole diameter:** Approximately 4.8 mm to 4.9 mm (typically designed to snugly accommodate or grip LEGO studs/cylindrical pins with a diameter of about 4.8 mm).

- **Hole spacing:** The standard distance between the centers of two adjacent holes is 8 mm. This is the core base dimension of the LEGO Technic grid system.
