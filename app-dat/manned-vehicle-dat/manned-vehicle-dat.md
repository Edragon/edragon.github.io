### Core Terminology Breakdown

* **Manned Vehicle / Crewed Vehicle:**
  * **Manned Vehicle:** The traditional technical counterpart to *unmanned*. It means a physical human operator or crew is onboard.
  * **Crewed Vehicle:** The modern, gender-neutral industry standard preferred by organizations like NASA and modern engineering firms over *manned*.

* **Human-Driven / Human-Operated Vehicle:**
  * **Human-Driven:** Used when highlighting that a human is making steering and acceleration decisions in real time (e.g., *human-driven karts vs. autonomous karts*).
  * **Human-Operated:** Broader term that covers both onboard drivers and direct manual control systems.

* **Manual Vehicle:**
  * Refers to vehicles requiring direct physical inputs (steering wheel, pedals, levers) from the operator without automated assistance or autonomous navigation.

---

### Terminology Comparison

| Term | Context & Nuance | Direct Opposite |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Manned / Crewed** | Focuses on **physical presence** of a human inside/on the vehicle. | Unmanned / Uncrewed |
| **Human-Driven** | Focuses on **active decision-making** by a human driver. | Autonomous / Self-driving |
| **Manual** | Focuses on **physical mechanical control** without automation. | Automated / Remote-controlled |

In the context of karting compared to autonomous robotics, calling it a **crewed vehicle** (for presence) or a **human-driven kart** (for control) provides the clearest contrast.
