RC Boat Movement Analysis (Differential Steering) - CW and CCW propellers

For an RC boat using **differential steering** with underwater dual motors (`left: clockwise/CW prop`, `right: counter-clockwise/CCW prop`), the direction of movement depends on the thrust generated by each motor. 

Assuming "Forward Rotation" generates forward thrust and "Reverse Rotation" generates reverse thrust:

---

## 1. Left Motor Forward + Right Motor Forward
* **Thrust:** Left motor pushes **FORWARD**, Right motor pushes **FORWARD**.
* **Movement:** **Straight Forward**.
* **Physics Note:** Using matched `CW and CCW propellers` cancels out hydrodynamic torque, keeping the boat moving in a straight line without counter-steering or tilting.

---

## 2. Left Motor Reverse + Right Motor Reverse
* **Thrust:** Left motor pushes **BACKWARD**, Right motor pushes **BACKWARD**.
* **Movement:** **Straight Reverse (Backward)**.
* **Physics Note:** Both motors produce balanced reverse thrust, pulling the vessel straight backward along its centerline.

---

## 3. Left Motor Forward + Right Motor Reverse
* **Thrust:** Left motor pushes **FORWARD**, Right motor pushes **BACKWARD**.
* **Movement:** **Sharp Right Turn / Clockwise Zero-Radius Pivot**.
* **Physics Note:** The push on the left combined with the pull on the right creates a strong **clockwise force couple**. If both motors spin at the same speed, the boat spins on its central axis.

---

## 4. Left Motor Reverse + Right Motor Forward
* **Thrust:** Left motor pushes **BACKWARD**, Right motor pushes **FORWARD**.
* **Movement:** **Sharp Left Turn / Counter-Clockwise Zero-Radius Pivot**.
* **Physics Note:** The push on the right and pull on the left creates a **counter-clockwise force couple**, causing a sharp yaw or spin to the left.

---

## Summary Matrix

| Left Motor  | Right Motor | Left Thrust | Right Thrust | Resulting Movement                      |
| :---------- | :---------- | :---------- | :----------- | :-------------------------------------- |
| **Forward** | **Forward** | Forward     | Forward      | **Straight Forward**                    |
| **Reverse** | **Reverse** | Backward    | Backward     | **Straight Reverse**                    |
| **Forward** | **Reverse** | Forward     | Backward     | **Right Turn / Clockwise Pivot**        |
| **Reverse** | **Forward** | Backward    | Forward      | **Left Turn / Counter-Clockwise Pivot** |
