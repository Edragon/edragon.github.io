To add lights to `a remote-controlled speedboat (rc boat)` while maintaining balance, you need to manage weight distribution, wind resistance, and electrical layout carefully. Because speedboats are extremely sensitive to weight shifts and center of gravity, follow these principles:

---

### 1. Weight Balance: Ensure Absolute Symmetry & Low Center of Gravity
Speedboats are sensitive to both lateral (left-right) and longitudinal (front-back) weight distribution:

*   **Longitudinal Balance (Front-to-Back):**
    *   The boat's center of gravity is typically **30% to 40%** from the stern.
    *   **Headlights:** Front-mounted lights are placed far from the center, magnifying their weight effect. Use **ultra-lightweight** micro LEDs or surface-mount LEDs. Keep heavier components like batteries or drivers close to the center of the boat, running thin wires to the front.
    *   **Tail/Navigation Lights:** Balance rear weights against any front additions, or use the main battery tray for longitudinal adjustments.
*   **Lateral Balance (Left-to-Right):**
    *   Left and right sides must have **nearly identical weight**. For example, pair a red port navigation light with a green starboard navigation light of matching power, casing, and wire weight.
    *   **Avoid Single-Sided Loads:** Never mount a heavy spotlight or battery on just one side, as this causes listing (tilting) during high-speed turns or straight runs, which can lead to capsizing.
*   **Wind Resistance & Height (Vertical Balance):**
    *   High speeds mean tall light brackets create wind resistance and a "sail effect," causing flips during sharp turns.
    *   **Flush/Streamlined Design:** Use low-profile, streamlined light housings mounted flush against the hull or cabin, avoiding tall brackets.

---

### 2. Electrical & Power Balance: Protecting Propulsion
Adding lights increases both physical weight and electrical load:

*   **Independent Power vs. Receiver Power:**
    *   **Low Current (Few LEDs):** Can draw power directly from the receiver (spare channel/servo port) or via a voltage regulator (BEC) from the main battery.
    *   **High Power (Bright Spotlights/Light Strips):** **Strongly recommend using an independent micro lithium battery (such as a 1S/2S pack)** dedicated solely to the lighting system. Tapping too much power from the main ESC battery can disrupt ESC performance and cause radio interference.
*   **Battery Placement:**
    *   If using an independent battery for lights, place it **strictly along the centerline in the lowest part of the hull**. This maintains lateral balance and lowers the overall center of gravity.

---

### 3. Recommended Lighting Schemes
*   **Scale Navigation Lights (Most Recommended & Best Balance):**
    *   **Layout:** `White headlight/mast light up front`, `red port light on the left`, `green starboard light on the right`, and `a white stern light`.
    *   **Advantages:** Multi-point distribution keeps individual components extremely light and symmetrical, preserving boat balance.
*   **Cabin Ambient / Hull Underglow Lights:**
    *   **Layout:** For semi-transparent hulls, place a waterproof LED strip **inside the center of the cabin**, or add waterproof underglow/transom lights near the waterline.
    *   **Advantages:** Lights are hidden inside or flush to the hull with zero wind resistance, concentrating weight at the exact center.
*   **Low-Profile Streamlined Headlights:**
    *   **Layout:** If front spotlights are necessary, use teardrop-shaped housings, install them **symmetrically as a pair** (or a centered dual micro-light module), and route wiring invisibly inside the hull.

---

### 4. Pro Tips for Assembly
*   **Waterproofing:** High-speed boats kick up massive spray. All hull penetrations and wire splices must be completely sealed with 704 silicone rubber or marine-grade waterproof sealant.
*   **Static & Dynamic Water Test:** Before running at full speed, gently place the boat in calm water to **check the waterline**. If one side sinks lower, the weight distribution is uneven and internal battery or wire positions must be adjusted.
