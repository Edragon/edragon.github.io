A **primary trainer** is an aircraft specifically designed for **student screening** and **initial flight training** (the foundational phase) in both military and civil aviation.

It is the very first powered aircraft a student pilot flies. Upon entering a flight school or air force academy, students complete their first flights, dual-instruction sessions, and first solo flights in a primary trainer.

---

## Core Characteristics and Design Requirements

To ensure zero-experience students can learn safely, primary trainers typically feature the following:

* **Excellent Flight Stability:** High margin for error, resistant to entering stalls or spins. If the student releases the controls, the aircraft generally tends to return to level flight automatically.
* **Gentle Control Response:** Control surfaces are not overly sensitive, preventing panic-induced over-correction by beginners.
* **Low Speeds and Short Takeoff/Landing (STOL) Capability:** Low cruise and landing speeds (typically lightweight propeller aircraft), requiring minimal runway length for frequent training circuits.
* **Side-by-Side or Tandem Seating:** Instructor and student share a cockpit (typically dual-control), allowing the instructor to take over instantly in critical moments.
* **Simple Maintenance and High Economy:** Sturdy construction (usually metal or composite materials), low fuel consumption, and easy ground servicing.

---

## Position in the Flight Training Pipeline

In a standard pilot training progression, trainers are categorized into tiers:

1. **Primary Trainer:** Learning basics like takeoffs, landings, straight-and-level flight, and fundamental turns (e.g., Cessna 172, Diamond DA20, CJ-6).
2. **Intermediate / Advanced Trainer:** Learning aerobatics, instrument flying (IFR), formation flying, and basic tactical maneuvers (e.g., K-8, T-6 Texan II).
3. **Jet / Type Transition:** Transitioning to high-performance jets or commercial airliners.

---

## Classic Examples

* **Military Classic:** The Chinese **CJ-6 (ChuJiao-6)**. A legendary propeller-driven primary trainer with a metal airframe and pneumatic retractable landing gear, renowned for its ruggedness and exceptional flight characteristics.
* **General & Civil Aviation Common Types:**
  * **Cessna 172:** The most mass-produced and widely used civil primary trainer in the world (high-wing design).
  * **Diamond DA20 / DA40:** Modern composite-material trainers featuring excellent visibility and low fuel consumption.
