RadioMaster Pocket CC2500 Version

YES, it works directly out of the box.

The CC2500 internal radio chip natively supports older legacy protocols including `FrSky D8 and D16`.  

How to pair them:

Turn on the radio and press/hold the MDL button to open Model Setup.

Scroll to Internal RF, set the protocol to FrSky, and the sub-type to D8 (D8 is much more reliable on SPI receivers than D16).

Plug your Mobula6 into Betaflight Configurator on your PC.

Go to the Receiver tab and click Bind Receiver (or run bind_rx in the CLI tab).

Scroll to [Bind] on your radio screen and press the scroll wheel.
