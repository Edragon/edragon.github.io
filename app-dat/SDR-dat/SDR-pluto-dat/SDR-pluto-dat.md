


# SDR-pluto-dat

- [[analog-device-dat]] - [[SDR-pluto-dat]] - [[SDR-dat]] - [[GNU-radio-dat]] - [[SDR-console-dat]]

The Analog Devices ADALM-Pluto (PlutoSDR) is a low-cost, portable software-defined radio (SDR) learning and development module.

Key Specifications & Features

- • RF Coverage: Officially covers 325 MHz to 3.8 GHz (though the AD9363 chip can typically be software-unlocked/expanded to cover 70 MHz to 6 GHz).
- • Bandwidth & Channels: Standard unit provides 1 transmit and 1 receive channel (half or full duplex) with up to 20 MHz of instantaneous bandwidth (and higher expanded bandwidths on modified/clone firmware).
- • Processor & FPGA: Built on the Analog Devices AD9363 RF transceiver and a Xilinx Zynq Z-7010 (or Z-7020 on upgraded variants) SoC/FPGA.
- • Connectivity: USB-powered (USB 2.0 OTG) with libiio driver support across Windows, macOS, and Linux. Community/upgraded boards (like Pluto+) also add onboard Gigabit Ethernet and Micro SD card boot slots.
- • Software Ecosystem: Works with MATLAB, Simulink, GNU Radio, IIO Oscilloscope, and SDR Console.

Common Use Cases

- Learning and experimenting with SDR concepts
- RF signal analysis and prototyping
- Amateur radio and satellite communications
- IoT and wireless protocol development
- Integration with GNU Radio and SDR Console for advanced signal processing
