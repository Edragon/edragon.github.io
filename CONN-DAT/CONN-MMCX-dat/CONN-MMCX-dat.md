# What is an MMCX Connector?

An **MMCX** (Micro-Miniature Coaxial) connector is a tiny, snap-on radio frequency (RF) coaxial connector commonly used in compact electronics, wireless modules, and radio communication equipment. 

---

### 1. Key Features
* **Tiny Footprint**: With a snap-on outer diameter of only about **2.4 mm**, it is significantly smaller than traditional SMA or RP-SMA connectors, making it ideal for space-constrained devices.
* **360-Degree Rotation**: MMCX connectors feature a lock-and-snap mechanism that allows the attached cable or antenna to **rotate 360 degrees** after being plugged in. This prevents cable strain and makes routing much easier inside tight enclosures.
* **Durability (Mating Cycles)**: Despite their miniature size, standard MMCX connectors are rated for around **500 mating cycles**, offering a reliable physical connection.
* **Frequency Range**: Typically supports high-frequency signals ranging from **DC up to 6 GHz** (and sometimes higher in precision versions), maintaining a characteristic impedance of **50 ohms**.

### 2. Common Applications
Because of its small size and reliable high-frequency performance, you will frequently find MMCX connectors in:
* **FPV Drones and Video Transmitters (VTX)**: Many modern digital and analog VTX units (such as DJI O3 Air Unit or various micro VTXs) use MMCX to connect the antenna to the board instead of bulky U.FL or SMA connectors.
* **Wi-Fi and Bluetooth Modules**: Embedded network cards and miniature router boards.
* **SDR (Software Defined Radio) & GPS Receivers**: Compact portable radio hardware and high-precision GNSS receivers.

### 3. Comparison with Other Common RF Connectors
* **vs. U.FL (IPEX)**: U.FL connectors are even smaller and flatter (often used inside smartphones or tiny micro whoop drones), but they are extremely fragile and meant for only a few insertion cycles. MMCX is much more mechanically robust and built for repeated plugging/unplugging.
* **vs. SMA / RP-SMA**: SMA connectors are large, threaded, and used on standard ground stations or large gear. MMCX is its micro-sized sibling designed for internal or ultra-compact external routing.
