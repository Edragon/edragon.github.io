* **Moisture and Oxidation Protection for Components and PCB**:
  * Store STM32 chips and PCB boards in a dry cabinet. If exposed to air too long (beyond the MSL level specified for moisture-sensitive devices), **baking** is mandatory before placement (typically 125 °C for 4–24 hours) to prevent "popcorning" or pin oxidation caused by moisture absorption.
