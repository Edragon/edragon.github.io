$0.65\text{ mm}$ pitch


Routing & Fanout: Because of the tight $0.65\text{ mm}$ ball pitch, escape routing typically requires micro-vias (laser vias) and a multi-layer stackup (at least 4 to 6 layers) to cleanly break out inner power planes and high-speed data lines (such as DDR and sensor MIPI/parallel interfaces).
