## voltage low rectifier 

The `MBR2045FCT` is a dual Schottky barrier rectifier diode configured with a common cathode.

Key Specifications

• Type: Schottky rectifying diode array (dual / common cathode)
• Max Repetitive Peak Reverse Voltage (\(V_{RRM}\)): 45 V
• Average Forward Current (\(I_{F}\)): 20 A total (10 A per diode leg)
• Package Type: TO-220F / ITO-220AB (insulated / isolated tab package)
• Features: Low power loss, high efficiency, guard ring for transient protection, and high current capability.
• Common Applications: Low-voltage, high-frequency inverters, free-wheeling diodes, and polarity protection circuits.
